The Daily Star (About Us)

The Daily Star began its journey on January 14, 1991, as Bangladesh saw the fall of an autocratic regime and embraced a new era of democracy. Driven by a commitment to independent media and press freedom, the paper has since evolved into one of the most trusted news sources in the country and is currently the largest circulating English-language daily.

The Daily Star was co-founded by Syed Mohammad Ali and Mahfuz Anam with the support of leading Bangladeshi businessmen, Azimur Rahman, A. S. Mahmud, Latifur Rahman, A. Rouf Chowdhury and Shamsur Rahman. Mahfuz Anam assumed the role of Editor in 1993 following the passing of SM Ali.

For over three decades, The Daily Star has remained steadfast in its pursuit of journalistic excellence. Today, we are a multi-platform news organisation, serving not only Bangladesh but also a global audience. Our dedication to growth and innovation ensures that The Daily Star remains a leading source of news and information for Bangladesh, and the world, for generations to come.

 

MISSION 

The Daily Star is committed to strengthening public understanding of democracy and holding power to account through journalism without fear or favour. Since its founding, we have remained unwavering in our pursuit of objective reporting and upholding journalistic integrity. Our commitment extends beyond just news reporting. Through investigations, in-depth reports, human-interest stories, multimedia features, and expert analysis we aim to spark conversation and inspire action. 

CORE VALUES

• Freedom of expression and of media: Our Editorial position is always upholding free media.

• Objectivity: We report the news accurately and truthfully, presenting facts without bias.

• Public Service: We serve the Bangladeshi people as a watchdog, holding those in power accountable.

• Championing Justice and Equality: We advocate for the rule of law, human rights, and gender equality.

• National Interest: We promote the best interests of Bangladesh on the national and global stage.

• Transparency & Accountability: We champion public accountability and transparency across all sectors of society.

 

CHAMPIONING BANGLADESHI EXCELLENCE

The Daily Star proudly recognizes outstanding individuals and organizations across various fields in Bangladesh. These prestigious awards celebrate achievement, inspire future generations, and set a positive example for society. . 

• O & A Level Awards: Held annually since 1999, The O & A Level Awards honour exceptional students who excel in their O and A Level exams and bring pride to Bangladesh on the global stage.

 

• Bangladesh Business Awards: In partnership with DHL, The Daily Star presents the Bangladesh Business Awards since 2000 to recognise excellence in business, foster entrepreneurship and elevate corporate management standards in Bangladesh.

 

• Bangladesh Sustainability Excellence Awards: Co-organised with CSR Window, the award recognises organisations and individuals leading the charge in social responsibility and community service, contributing to achieving the Sustainable Development Goals.

 

• The Daily Star ICT Awards: Since 2016, this award has been recognising and celebratinggroundbreaking achievements in the field of information and communication technology.

 

• The Daily Star-IPDC Unsung Women Nation Builders Awards: Partnering with IPDC Finance, the Unsung Women Nation Builders Award, identifies and champions women from grassroot communities who are defying obstacles and driving positive change.

 

• Stellar Women Award: Co-organised with bti, the Stellar Women Award recognises and empowers promising professional women from different fields, who are making significant contributions to society and nation-building.

 

• Nirbhaya: Launched after the Covid-19 pandemic this award aims to celebrate women and girls who are championing advancement of transformative technology and challenging gendered social norms.

 

• Blender's Choice - The Daily Star OTT and Digital Content Awards: To honour artists, content creators, influencer, innovators, and organisations who are contributing to Over-the-Top and digital content industry, The Daily Star hosts the OTT and Digital Content Awards in partnership with Blender's Choice.

 

OUR TEAM

The Daily Star is a collaborative effort driven by over 300 dedicated individuals. Our team comprises a diverse range of professionals who work together to bring you the news and stories that matter in both print and digital formats.

At the heart of our newsroom are our journalists and reporters, dedicated to journalistic excellence in covering national and international news in English and Bangla. Beyond the breaking news, our Editorial and Opinion section, and Lifestyle, Technology and Youth, and Multimedia features provide context, in-depth analysis and engaging stories.

Ensuring the smooth delivery of the news requires a dedicated team behind the scenes. Our essential support staff working in business development finance, HR, circulation, production, and IT, builder partnerships manage resources, ensure efficient operations, and navigate the technical landscape – all to get the news to you. While each member has their specific role, they are united by a common thread: a commitment to journalism, innovation, and serving the Bangladeshi people.
