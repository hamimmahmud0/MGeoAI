# T03 — Reframe the LLM-adjudication claim

Status: todo
Depends on: T02 (final wording depends on baseline results)

## Goal
Do not claim an accuracy benefit from LLM adjudication: the heuristic-only
ablation matches the hybrid F1. Keep and sharpen the distinction between
deterministic screening and LLM-based adjudication, and keep acknowledging the
null result openly.

## Done when
- [ ] Every claim about LLM benefit in abstract, intro, results, discussion and
      conclusion is reviewed and reworded to what the data supports.
- [ ] The deterministic-screening vs LLM-adjudication distinction is stated
      clearly (what each stage does, what the guards enforce).
- [ ] If any benefit is argued (e.g. explanations, fusion quality), it is backed
      by a measurement, not asserted.
- [ ] Section list of edits recorded here for the rebuttal letter.
