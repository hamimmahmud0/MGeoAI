# Buet student killed in Purbachal road accident

## Article or Post

<!-- block:blk_6c4b277ee02bc1d8 -->
Two of his classmates have been injured and hospitalized

<!-- block:blk_e2b82ad4318bc385 -->
A student of Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (Buet) was killed and two others were injured as a car hit their motorcycle in Purbachal’s 300 Feet area in Rupganj, Narayanganj early on Friday.

<!-- block:blk_378fcf2db7a84271 -->
The deceased was Motasim Masud, 22, a second-year computer science and engineering student.

<!-- block:blk_b2dcbe40594bc9b2 -->
Injured Amit Saha, 22, and Mehedi Hasan, 21, are from the same department.

<!-- block:blk_4e665c13e03224cd -->
Police later detained the car’s driver in connection with the fatal crash.

<!-- block:blk_3202279f796016de -->
According to their classmate Faiyaz, Motasim lived in Kalabagan, while Amit and Mehedi are residents of Buet’s Ahsanullah Hall.

<!-- block:blk_d6611243ad9296d8 -->
Rupganj police station Officer-in-Charge (OC) Liakat Ali said Motasim had died on the spot, adding that Amit and Mehedi had been admitted to Kurmitola General Hospital.

<!-- block:blk_3aa3d816e3132de3 -->
The car’s driver, Sadman, had been detained, the OC said, mentioning that two others who had also been in the vehicle had been taken to the police station for questioning.
