# Tongi students lift Dhaka-Mymensingh Highway blockade

## Article or Post

<!-- block:blk_5526180aba254378 -->
Students ended their blockade of the Dhaka-Mymensingh Highway in Gazipur's Tongi around 3pm today after police assured them that their demands for foot overbridges would be met.

<!-- block:blk_e859d8565c09fcc4 -->
The students had blocked the highway around 11am at the Asia Pump and Gazipur bus stand, protesting the death of Yasin, 17, a class nine student of Tamirul Millat Kamil Madrasa, in a road accident.

<!-- block:blk_7be65f6919c5737d -->
The blockade brought buses on the Dhaka-Mymensingh, Dhaka-Kishoreganj and Dhaka-Tangail routes to a standstill, causing suffering for thousands of passengers.

<!-- block:blk_e356f8e78fb19923 -->
Shafikur Rahman, an inspector of Gazipur Traffic Police, said the students demanded the immediate construction of foot overbridges at the two locations.

<!-- block:blk_cf5ed224f2b9d8cc -->
Protesters were seen carrying banners and festoons, including one that read, “Foot overbridges must be constructed immediately.”

<!-- block:blk_d148c4c5af0ef905 -->
Personnel from Tongi East and Tongi West police stations were deployed at the scene and tried to persuade the students to withdraw the blockade.

<!-- block:blk_c65ff200bcb93886 -->
Although the blockade has been lifted, traffic on the highway has yet to return to normal, with congestion persisting following the hours-long protest.

<!-- block:blk_41464103cfd56c9b -->
Attempts to contact Tamirul Millat Kamil Madrasa Principal Mohammad Hifzur Rahman by phone were unsuccessful as he did not answer the calls.
