## Summary

A fatal road crash occurred early on 20 December 2024 at a police checkpoint near Neela Market intersection on Purbachal 300 Feet Road in Rupganj, Narayanganj. A passenger car struck a stationary motorcycle carrying three BUET students, killing 22-year-old Muhtasim (Mohtassim) Masud, a second-year CSE student, and seriously injuring his two friends, Amit Saha and Mehedi Hasan [ev_7a9911d957c3b042][ev_f6979130e8086261][ev_3cb89b4791adcc04].
