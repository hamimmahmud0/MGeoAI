# BUET student killed in motorcycle-car collision at Purbachal police checkpoint

> Evidence-fusion output. This record does not independently verify the incident.

## Summary

On the early hours of 20 December 2024, a fatal road crash occurred at a police checkpoint on Purbachal 300 Feet Road in Rupganj, Narayanganj, Bangladesh. A passenger car struck a stationary motorcycle carrying three BUET students who were stopped for questioning. The collision killed 22-year-old Muhtasim (Mohtassim) Masud on the spot and injured his two friends, Amit Saha and Mehedi Hasan [ev_7e2b75efeb5007b6, ev_f6979130e8086261]. Multiple news outlets report the car was speeding and recklessly driven; one video headline alleges the accused were intoxicated, but this remains an unconfirmed claim [ev_07a4549765a2d7a1, ev_7a9911d957c3b042]. The crash location is consistently described as the Nila Market intersection on the 300 Feet Road, though broader area references (Purbachal Uposhohor) also appear [ev_850f0b4bcf2418e4, ev_4868bd0517e733cd]. Police at the checkpoint were present; the victim's body was sent to Shaheed Suhrawardy Medical College Hospital for postmortem, and both vehicles were seized [ev_1f834f7777ccf794]. A case was filed under the Road Transport Act 2018 by the victim's father, and three accused were later sent to jail by a Narayanganj court [ev_6f614cdd3f691d5e, ev_23e903df4479a2d7]. Six independent sources cover the incident. Key unresolved issues include the legal status of the intoxication allegation, the precise crash mechanism, and the current condition of the injured students [ev_17797e3df968c79a].
