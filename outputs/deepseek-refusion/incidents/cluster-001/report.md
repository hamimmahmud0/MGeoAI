# Fatal crash at Nila Market checkpoint in Purbachal kills BUET student Muhtasim Masud

> Evidence-fusion output. This record does not independently verify the incident.

## Summary

On 20 December 2024, at about 3:00 am, a car reportedly struck a stationary motorcycle at a police checkpoint/check post at Nila Market intersection on Purbachal 300 Feet Road in Rupganj upazila, Narayanganj. Reports identify the rider as Muhtasim Masud, 22, a second-year BUET computer science student, who died at the scene; two classmates, Amit Saha and Mehedi Hasan, were injured [ev_7a9911d957c3b042, ev_7e2b75efeb5007b6, ev_4c5ab42977ec172a, ev_c72db83917462014]. The reported collision sequence is a car hitting the stopped motorcycle, but the ATN video analysis shows only aftermath damage to one passenger car and police escort of three men, not the impact [ev_07a4549765a2d7a1]. Sources describe the car as speeding, and an on-screen headline alleges the accused were intoxicated; these remain reported allegations [ev_17797e3df968c79a, ev_07a4549765a2d7a1]. No explicit traffic blockage or congestion was reported; an image at Rupganj police station shows no crash damage or lane obstruction [ev_33667123810c930a]. A case under the Road Transport Act was filed by the victim's father, police arrested three people, and a Narayanganj court later sent the three accused to jail; the body was sent for postmortem and the motorcycle and car were seized [ev_6f614cdd3f691d5e, ev_9a606d918d721b9b, ev_23e903df4479a2d7, ev_1f834f7777ccf794]. Coverage spans at least four independent reporting sources plus a Dhaka Tribune repost. The location is area-level: the supplied coordinate is a Nila Market gazetteer centroid with about 1.5 km uncertainty, while some reports only name Purbachal or Narayanganj. The main unresolved uncertainty is whether the speed and intoxication allegations are substantiated beyond the reported claims.
