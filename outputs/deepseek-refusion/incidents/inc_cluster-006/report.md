# Two passenger buses collide on Dhaka–Sylhet highway in Kashikapan, Osmaninagar, Sylhet

> Evidence-fusion output. This record does not independently verify the incident.

## Summary

On 7 August 2026, two passenger buses collided head-on in the Kashikapan area of the Dhaka–Sylhet highway in Osmaninagar, Sylhet, according to reporting from Prothom Alo [ev_99685340cfb96e6d] and The Daily Star [ev_8675a512af387063]. Police and eyewitnesses cited by Prothom Alo identified the vehicles as a Sylhet-bound Bengal Paribahan bus and a Dhaka-bound Unique Paribahan bus [ev_99685340cfb96e6d]. The Daily Star cited witnesses saying the Bengal bus attempted to overtake a pickup, veered onto the right side, and hit the Unique bus, which fell into a roadside ditch [ev_e7e4274f75924b4d]; a social-media video reportedly shows loss of control [ev_8675a512af387063]. The exact location is only known at area level; the supplied point is a representative centroid with about 3 km uncertainty. Casualty counts conflict: Prothom Alo reports eight killed and at least 25 injured [ev_a50c19158fca4347], while The Daily Star reports nine passengers of the Unique bus died and at least 24 were injured [ev_f4f6bf3ae3a5ae94]. Injured passengers were reportedly rescued and taken to Sylhet MAG Osmani Medical College Hospital [ev_03c6593b6ff5558e]. The identities of the dead and injured were not immediately known [ev_189d525e6f97c130]. Two independent sources cover this crash. No traffic closure or delay details were supplied.
